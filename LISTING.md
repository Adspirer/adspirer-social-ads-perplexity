## Short description

Create Meta and TikTok ad campaigns. Research competitors, develop creative, analyze results, and optimize spending—with your approval.

## Long description

Adspirer Social Ads connects Perplexity to your advertising accounts through a hosted MCP server. It covers
Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok Ads. It can change ad accounts, and only with your approval: campaigns are built paused, every change is shown first and read back afterwards. The agent never purchases or upgrades a subscription on your behalf. Sign-in
is browser OAuth with your Adspirer account; ad accounts are connected at
https://adspirer.ai/connections. An Adspirer account and an applicable plan are required.

## Example prompts

- "What ads are my competitors running on Meta right now?"
- "Which Meta ad sets show creative fatigue, and what should I refresh?"
- "Plan a TikTok campaign for our new product and build it paused for my approval."

## Getting listed

Perplexity has no self-serve directory for third-party connectors. Users add Adspirer Social Ads as a
custom connector with the URL above. To ask for it to be offered as a managed connector, email
api@perplexity.ai:

```
Subject: Managed connector request: Adspirer Social Ads

Hi Perplexity team,

We'd like to request Adspirer Social Ads as a managed connector.

Create Meta and TikTok ad campaigns. Research competitors, develop creative, analyze results, and optimize spending—with your approval.

- MCP server: https://mcp.adspirer.com/social-ads (Streamable HTTP, browser OAuth with PKCE)
- Platforms: Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok Ads
- Writes require user approval; campaigns are created paused.
- Logo: https://storage.googleapis.com/adspirer-images-v1/brand/adspirer-logo-square.png

you need from us for review.

Abhi
```
